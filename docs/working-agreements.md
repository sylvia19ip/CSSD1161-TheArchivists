### Work Based:

## Response Times:
- Best Etiquette: within 24 hours
- Acceptable Etiquette: within 2 - 3 days
- Extenuating Circumstances: within 1 week
- Bad Etiquette: 1+ week(s)

## Meeting Etiquette:
### Etiquette:
- Best Etiquette: Early/On Time
- Acceptable Etiquette: Up to 10 minutes late with prior notification to the group
- Extenuating Circumstances: Not arriving/arriving very late with prior notification
- Bad Etiquette: Not arriving with no communication
### Timeline: 
- During classes (Every Tuesday)
- After classes (Every Tuesday as needed)
- Outside classes (When scheduled) 

## PR Tone:
- Professional and concise
- Titles should be short
- Descriptions should adequately summarize changes
